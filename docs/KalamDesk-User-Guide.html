Part 1

Start here

Everything you need on your very first day: your invitation, signing in, and finding your way around.

01

Accept your invitation and choose a password

EveryoneAbout 2 min

When a founder adds you, KalamDesk emails you a Welcome to KalamDesk message. The link in it works once and expires after 3 days.

  1. New password. Choose at least 8 characters. A short sentence you will remember is better than a clever word, for example blue-chai-at-6pm.
  2. Confirm password. Type the same password again so a typo cannot lock you out.
  3. Set password. Click it. You then see a Sign in button, which takes you to the sign-in page.
Good to know
  • The link expired or stopped working? Ask your founder to open People → your name → Edit and send a new invitation.
  • Never share your password. Nobody at the company will ever ask you for it.
02

Sign in on any day

EveryoneAbout 1 min

Open the KalamDesk address your company gave you in any browser, on a laptop or a phone. Bookmark it, or add it to your phone's home screen.

  1. Email. Use your official company email, the same one the invitation was sent to.
  2. Password. The one you chose. If you turned on two-step sign-in, you will be asked for a 6-digit code on the next screen.
  3. Sign in. Click it, or press Enter on your keyboard.
  4. Forgot password? Click it, type your email, and we send a link to choose a new one. It works once and lasts 1 hour.
Good to know
  • Five wrong passwords in a row pauses sign-in for 10 minutes. Wait, then try again, or use Forgot password?.
  • You are signed out automatically after 12 hours, so sign in again each day.
03

Read and accept the privacy notice

EveryoneAbout 1 min

The first time you sign in, a short notice appears. It tells you what KalamDesk stores about you and why.

  1. The notice. We keep your name, contact details, work records and task performance only to assign work, measure delivery fairly and run incentives. Bank and ID details are encrypted and only founders can open them.
  2. I understand and agree. Click it to continue. You cannot use the app until you do.
Good to know
  • You can download everything we hold about you, or ask for a correction or deletion, any time from Settings → Your data and privacy.
04

Your first day: what you will see

EveryoneAbout 3 min

A brand-new account is quiet, and that is normal. This is My Day, your home screen. It fills up as soon as you are given work.

  1. I'm in. Tap this when you start work. It records your attendance for the day. Tap I'm out when you finish.
  2. Your 30-second stand-up. Click Fill it in to tell the team what you finished and what is pending. Do this every working day.
  3. Weekly goal. A gentle target of how many tasks to finish this week. The bar fills as you finish work.
  4. Plan my day. Nothing assigned yet? Add your own tasks here. Self-started work shows up on the founders' desk and earns bonus points once accepted.
Good to know
  • Your first three actions on day one: tap I'm in, file your first stand-up, and add one task of your own.
05

Find your way around: the left menu

EveryoneAbout 3 min

The dark menu on the left is on every screen (on a phone it is the bar along the bottom, which scrolls sideways). It has three groups.

  1. Your organisation at the top. If you work for more than one organisation and have been given access, click it to switch.
  2. My Day. Your home screen: what is due, what is overdue, and what to do next.
  3. Scores. The leaderboard: points this week, this month and all time.
  4. People. A names-only directory: your colleagues' names, roles and departments. Contact details are visible to managers and founders only, and you only ever see people in your own organisation.
  5. Group headings. *My work*, *Performance* and *Company* group everything. Items you cannot use are simply not shown to you.
  6. + New task. Add a task from any screen. Tip: press N anywhere on your keyboard.
  7. The bell. Your notifications: tasks assigned to you, comments, kudos and reminders. A red dot means something is new.
  8. Settings and Sign out. Bottom left. Your level, points and a progress bar to the next level are shown just above.
Good to know
  • Your level goes up as you earn points. It is a bit of fun, and it also unlocks badges.
Part 2

Your working day

The routine that keeps you, your manager and your scores on track.

06

My Day: your home screen

EveryoneAbout 3 min

Open My Day first thing every morning. Everything due today, and anything overdue, is here.

  1. The greeting banner. Shows your streak (days in a row you filed a stand-up), points this week and your level. The ring shows tasks done today.
  2. I'm in / I'm out. Records your attendance. It is a single tap.
  3. Focus now. The one task to do next. Mark done finishes it. Open details shows the full task.
  4. The round tick. The quick way to finish any task in your list.
  5. The status menu. Change a task between To Do, In Progress, Blocked, In Review and Done without opening it.
  6. Weekly goal. How many tasks you have finished against this week's goal.
  7. Your week in numbers. Last week's points, tasks done, on-time rate, stand-ups and kudos, and your rank among your peers.
Good to know
  • Red Overdue items come first. If something is stuck, set its status to Blocked and say why, so it does not count against you as silently late.
  • Some tasks need proof before they can be finished. You will be asked to add a note or file.
07

Add a task in ten seconds

EveryoneAbout 2 min

Click + New task, or press N anywhere. Type naturally; KalamDesk reads it for you.

  1. Type the task in plain words. Example: Raise invoice for Sunrise School @Pooja P1 tomorrow 5pm. It understands @name for who, P1 to P4 for priority, and words like today, tomorrow, tue or 5pm for the deadline.
  2. For: me. Shows who the task is for. Employees and interns can add tasks for themselves. Asking someone else to do something sends them a *request* they can see.
  3. Priority. P1 is urgent, P2 high, P3 normal, P4 when free. P3 is the default.
  4. Add task. Saves it, or press Enter.
  5. Full form. Want a description, checklist, location or exact date? Open the full form instead.
Good to know
  • Self-started tasks are marked *Self-initiated*. When a founder accepts one, you earn an initiative bonus.
08

Open a task: status, checklist, files and comments

EveryoneAbout 4 min

Click any task title to open it. This is where the work gets done and recorded.

  1. Status. To Do, In Progress, Blocked, In Review or Done. Moving to Blocked asks for a reason, and the person who assigned it is told straight away.
  2. Add a step. Break the task into a checklist. Tick steps off as you go.
  3. Attach file. Photos, PDFs, Office files and CSV, up to 10 MB each.
  4. Comment box. Ask questions or give updates. Type @name to mention a colleague and they are notified.
  5. Activity. An automatic history of who changed what and when. It cannot be edited.
Good to know
  • Tasks that need approval go to In Review. A manager or founder approves or sends them back with a note.
  • Finishing a task on time earns the most points; finishing late earns fewer. See *Scores*.
09

File your daily stand-up

EveryoneAbout 2 min

Every working day, spend two minutes here. It keeps your streak alive, and it means your manager never has to chase you for a status.

  1. What did you finish today? Short and specific beats long: 20 calls, 2 school visits, invoice for Sunrise School.
  2. What is pending? What you will pick up tomorrow.
  3. Any obstacles? Anything slowing you down. Founders see these, and it is the fastest way to get help.
  4. Save. The button reads Update again if you have already filed today, and you can edit as often as you like until midnight.
Good to know
  • Missed a day? The streak resets, but nothing is lost. Start a new one today.
  • Short on time? If your company has turned on WhatsApp, send UPDATE <what you did> to the KalamDesk number instead.
10

Board, List and Calendar: three ways to see your work

EveryoneAbout 4 min

Board shows tasks as cards in columns. Drag a card to another column to change its status.

  1. Filters. Narrow by priority or tag. Employees and interns see only their own work. Managers and founders also get Person and Department filters, to see their team or everyone.
  2. A task card. Click it to open the task. The coloured edge shows priority.
  3. To Do column. Work that has not started.
  4. Done column. Finished work. Drop a card here to finish it.
Good to know
  • Prefer a table? Use List. Prefer a month view? Use Calendar (next two screens).
11

List: sort and filter every task

EveryoneAbout 2 min

List shows the same tasks as a sortable table. It is the best view for searching and for bulk work.

  1. Priority filter. Also filter by tag, then click Apply. (Managers and founders can filter by person and department too.)
  2. Apply. Run your filters.
  3. Column headings. Click TASK, PRIORITY, STATUS or DUE to sort.
  4. A row. Click to open the task.
Good to know
  • Managers and founders can tick several rows and change status, priority or assignee for all of them at once.
12

Calendar: deadlines by date

EveryoneAbout 1 min

See every deadline laid out across the month.

  1. Previous month.
  2. Next month.
  3. A task. Click to open it. Overdue tasks are shown in red.
Good to know
  • Want these deadlines in Google, Apple or Outlook calendar? See Settings → Calendar link.
13

Notifications: the bell

EveryoneAbout 1 min

KalamDesk tells you when something needs you: a new task, a comment, a task sent back, a deadline in 2 hours, or kudos from a colleague.

  1. The bell. A red dot means something is new. Click to open the list.
  2. A notification. Click it to jump straight to the task or page. It is then marked as read.
Good to know
  • Important notifications also arrive by email, and by WhatsApp if you turned that on in Settings.
Part 3

Leave, visits, leads and expenses

The everyday business tools. Use them instead of messages and spreadsheets, so everything lives in one place.

14

Ask for leave and mark attendance

EveryoneAbout 3 min

Open Leave from the menu. Your manager and the founders are told as soon as you ask.

  1. I'm in / I'm out. Your daily attendance. Tap I'm in when you start and I'm out when you finish.
  2. Type of leave. Casual, Sick, Unpaid or Other.
  3. Cover person. Choose a colleague to cover for you. New tasks given to you while you are away are shared with them, so nothing sits idle.
  4. Dates. Choose the first and last day. Leave is counted in calendar days.
  5. Ask for leave. Sends the request. You will be notified when it is approved or declined.
  6. Cancel leave. Changed your plans? Cancel a pending or approved request here.
Good to know
  • Ask as early as you can. Leave longer than 60 days needs a conversation with a founder.
  • While you are on approved leave, your name shows an *Away until* tag on the founders' desk.
15

Record a school visit

EveryoneAbout 3 min

For field work such as school visits, open the visit task right after you finish. Two minutes now saves a long phone call later.

  1. Location. The place from the task. Tap it to open the address in Google Maps.
  2. How did it go? Great, Good, Needs follow-up or Problem. A Problem alerts the founders immediately.
  3. What happened? Who you met, what you showed, how students reacted.
  4. Save visit report. You can come back and edit it.
Good to know
  • Add photos with Attach file on the same task. The Visits page in the menu lists every report.
  • Fill in How many people attended and Follow-up needed too. They feed the reports founders see.
16

Track your leads

EveryoneAbout 4 min

Leads is your list of schools and contacts you are working to win. Log every call so nothing is forgotten.

  1. Contact name. Who you spoke to. Only name and, usually, the school are required.
  2. School or organisation.
  3. Phone (and email, deal size and source are optional). Tap a phone number later to call.
  4. Add lead. Saves it as a *New* lead.
  5. Filters. *Follow-up due* shows who you promised to call back today.
  6. Log a call. Record the outcome (connected, no answer, call back, not interested, wrong number) and set a follow-up date.
Good to know
  • Move a lead along its stages: New → Contacted → Demo → Proposal → Won or Lost, from the drop-down on the lead.
  • You see your own leads. Managers see their team's, and founders see everything.
17

Claim an expense

EveryoneAbout 3 min

Spent your own money on work? Claim it here and attach the receipt.

  1. What was it for? For example Auto fare to a school visit.
  2. Amount (₹). Whole rupees.
  3. Category. Travel, Fuel, Food, Stationery or Other.
  4. Receipt. A photo or PDF up to 5 MB. Take the photo while you still have the paper.
  5. Submit claim. A founder is notified. You can withdraw a claim until it is decided.
Good to know
  • The date of spending cannot be in the future.
  • A rejected claim always shows the reason. An approved claim shows as Paid once the money has been sent.
Part 4

Scores, rewards and recognition

How good work is measured, celebrated and rewarded.

18

Scores: the leaderboard

EveryoneAbout 3 min

Points reward finishing work, finishing it on time, and doing harder work. Employees and interns are ranked separately, so it is a fair comparison.

  1. Time range. This week, this month or all time.
  2. Employees or Interns. Choose which group to see.
  3. How points are calculated. Open this for the exact formula. Hover any score to see how it was worked out.
Good to know
  • Default points: P1 = 10, P2 = 7, P3 = 5, P4 = 3, multiplied for difficulty (1× easy to 2.2× hardest). On time = full points, up to 24 hours late = 70%, later = 40%. Founders can change these numbers.
  • Penalties are small and explained: a missed recurring activity costs 3 points, and a task left overdue for 3+ days without a blocker reason costs 2. Marking a task Blocked with a reason avoids the penalty.
19

Challenges, badges, streaks and kudos

EveryoneAbout 3 min

The fun part. It is there to make good habits visible.

  1. Your badges. Earned for milestones such as your first task, a 10-task club, and streaks. Locked badges show what to aim for next.
  2. Streak board. Who has filed stand-ups the most days in a row.
  3. Give someone kudos. Pick a colleague and a quick message, such as *Saved the day!*, or write your own.
  4. Send kudos. They get a notification, and it appears on the Kudos wall.
Good to know
  • Founders can also launch seasonal challenges, a team goal with a prize, such as a Diwali sprint. The progress bar is at the top of this page.
  • On the day, your team's birthdays and work anniversaries are celebrated on My Day.
20

Incentives: what you can earn

EveryoneAbout 2 min

Incentives are cash rewards for consistent, on-time work. Founders approve every payment.

  1. Your history. Every award you have received or are waiting on, by month.
  2. How to earn them. The current rules, for example *at least 40 points and 90% on time in a month*.
Good to know
  • Nothing is paid automatically. At the start of each month the founders review who qualified and approve.
  • Rules differ for employees and interns. The rules shown here are the ones that apply to you.
21

Referrals: bring someone in

EveryoneAbout 2 min

Know a school, a franchise partner or a candidate we should talk to? Refer them and earn the reward.

  1. Programme. Pick what you are referring. The reward is shown next to it.
  2. Their name.
  3. Contact. A phone number or email.
  4. Submit referral. A founder follows up. When it converts, the reward is added to your incentives.
Good to know
  • Add a note: how you know them and the best time to call. It helps us succeed.
Part 5

Settings and security

Your account, your privacy and how KalamDesk reaches you.

22

Install KalamDesk on your phone

EveryoneAbout 2 min

KalamDesk works as an app. Once installed, it opens from your home screen in one tap, full screen, with no browser bar, and the icon shows a red number when you have unread notifications.

Good to know
  • Android (Chrome or Edge): open KalamDesk, tap the Install app button at the top of the screen (or More → Install KalamDesk as an app), then tap Install. If you do not see it, open the browser menu (three dots) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): open KalamDesk in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  • Laptop (Chrome or Edge): click the small install icon at the right of the address bar, or the Install app button in KalamDesk.
  • On a phone the bottom bar has three shortcuts (My Day, Stand-up, Board), a big + to add a task, and More, which opens every other page, your level, Settings and Sign out.
  • Press and hold the KalamDesk icon on Android to jump straight to Add a task, My Day or Daily update.
  • If your phone loses its connection, KalamDesk shows an offline notice. Changes are only saved when you are back online.
23

Settings: your account in one page

EveryoneAbout 4 min

Click Settings at the bottom of the left menu.

  1. Change password. Enter your current password and a new one (8+ characters).
  2. Calendar link. Click Show my calendar link and paste the private address into Google, Apple or Outlook calendar. Your deadlines then show up there. Keep the link private.
  3. Birthday. Optional. Choose a day and month (never a year) and your team can cheer your day.
  4. WhatsApp alerts. Enter your mobile number and tick the box to get alerts on WhatsApp. We never send a message without your tick.
  5. Download my data. Everything we hold about you in one file.
  6. Security. Turn on two-step sign-in (next page).
Good to know
  • Ask for a correction or Ask for deletion sends a request to your founders. This is your right under India's DPDP Act.
24

Two-step sign-in (strongly recommended for founders)

EveryoneAbout 5 min

Two-step sign-in asks for a 6-digit code from your phone as well as your password. Founders can open bank details, so founders should turn this on today.

  1. Set up. From Settings → Security click Set up two-step sign-in.
  2. Scan the QR code. In Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator, choose *Scan a QR code*.
  3. Cannot scan? Type the long key shown here into the app by hand.
  4. Type the 6-digit code the app shows now.
  5. Turn on. You are then shown 8 recovery codes. Save them somewhere safe (a password manager or paper). Each works once if you lose your phone.
Good to know
  • Locked out with no phone and no recovery codes? Another founder can switch your two-step sign-in off from your profile.
  • Revealing someone's bank details always asks you to confirm your password (and a code) again.
Part 6

For managers and founders

Running a team: assign, watch, approve and reward. Employees and interns do not see these screens.

25

The Founder's Desk: your whole team at a glance

Managers & foundersAbout 4 min

Open Desk every morning. Managers see their own team. Founders see everybody in the organisation.

  1. Health tiles. *Needs attention*, *Keep an eye*, *On track*, and how many stand-ups are in today.
  2. Self-initiated work to review. Tasks people started themselves. Open one to accept it (which earns the person a bonus) or leave it.
  3. A person's card. Overdue, blocked and open counts, today's top task, and when they last filed a stand-up. Click the name for their full page.
  4. Nudge. One friendly reminder to that person, at most once per day. It arrives as a notification, email and WhatsApp if on.
  5. Dashboard and the rest of the menu. Leads see extra items: Desk, Recurring, Dashboard, Stand-ups and Templates.
Good to know
  • Red means someone is overdue or has had no stand-up. Use Nudge before you message. It is kinder, and it is recorded.
26

People: your directory

Managers & foundersAbout 2 min

People lists everyone in your organisation, grouped by role.

  1. + Add person. Founders only. Starts the onboarding below.
  2. Departments. Founders only. Create and rename teams.
  3. A person. Click the name to see their open tasks and recent activity.
  4. edit. Founders only. Change a role, department, manager, or deactivate the account.
Good to know
  • People who leave are deactivated, never deleted. Their history is kept, and they cannot sign in.
27

Add a new employee or intern

FoundersAbout 4 min

Founders only. This is the first step of onboarding. KalamDesk emails the person an invitation to choose their own password. You never see or set it.

  1. Name. As they should appear to the team.
  2. Email. Their official company email. An email can belong to only one organisation.
  3. Role. *Intern* and *Employee* work on their own tasks. *Manager* leads a team. *Founder* sees everything in the organisation, including bank details.
  4. Department. Their team. Create more under *Departments*.
  5. Office. Hyderabad, Warangal, Remote or Other.
  6. Reports to. Their manager. This decides who approves their leave and sees their work.
  7. Add person and send invite. They receive the email immediately. Resend it any time from their profile if it is lost.
Good to know
  • For interns, also set the start and end dates further down the form. The team is reminded when an internship is about to end.
  • Fill in emergency contact and joining date too. The joining date powers work-anniversary celebrations.
28

A person's page

Managers & foundersAbout 2 min

Click anyone's name for a quick view of how they are doing.

  1. Edit. Founders can change details, role or reporting manager.
  2. Open tasks. Everything on their plate, with deadlines and status. You can change a status from here.
  3. Nudge. A friendly reminder on a single task.
  4. Recently done. What they finished lately.
Good to know
  • Founders also see bank details and ID documents (encrypted) on the Edit page. Each reveal asks for a password check and is written to the audit log.
29

Departments

FoundersAbout 2 min

Departments are the teams in your organisation. Each organisation has its own set.

  1. Name.
  2. Colour. Shown on tags and charts.
  3. Add.
  4. Rename / colour.
  5. Delete. Only possible when nobody and no task uses it.
Good to know
  • Examples: Accounts, BD, Marketing, Training, Tech. An NGO might use Programs and Outreach.
30

Recurring activities

Managers & foundersAbout 4 min

Anything that repeats: weekly school visits, Monday reports, month-start invoicing. KalamDesk creates each task for you at the right time.

  1. Activity. The task title that will repeat.
  2. Repeats. Weekly on chosen days, every weekday, or monthly.
  3. Days. Choose which days of the week.
  4. Assigned to. Tick everyone responsible. Each person gets their own task and is scored separately.
  5. Create recurring activity. Saves the rule. You can pause or edit it later.
Good to know
  • A task that is not done by the end of its day is marked Missed, closed, and a small penalty applies. The person and you are told.
  • Managers can only assign recurring work to their own team.
31

Templates: saved task shapes

Managers & foundersAbout 2 min

Make a template for work you create again and again, such as a school visit with its checklist.

  1. + New template. Give it a name, title, priority and checklist.
  2. Edit.
  3. Delete.
Good to know
  • When creating a task, choose a template and everything is filled in for you.
32

Dashboard: numbers for the week or month

Managers & foundersAbout 3 min

See how the team is performing, then share or export it.

  1. Weekly or Monthly.
  2. Export CSV. For a spreadsheet.
  3. Publish snapshot. Saves a read-only copy with a private link you can share, or download as PDF.
  4. Workload per person. Open tasks for each person. Uneven bars mean uneven work.
  5. Top performers. The current leaders.
Good to know
  • Every chart has View as table underneath for screen readers and for copying numbers.
33

Stand-ups: who has filed

Managers & foundersAbout 1 min

One page with everyone's daily update, newest first. Obstacles are highlighted so you can help.

Good to know
  • Use the arrows to move between days. Missing stand-ups are listed so you can nudge them.
34

Approve expense claims

FoundersAbout 2 min

Founders approve every expense. Claims waiting for you appear at the top.

  1. All claims. Open the receipt by clicking it.
  2. Approve. Then, once you have paid, click Mark paid.
  3. Reject. You must give a reason. The employee sees it.
  4. Download CSV. Send it to your accountant.
Good to know
  • You cannot approve your own claim unless you are the only founder.
35

Approve leave

Managers & foundersAbout 2 min

Open Leave. Requests from your team are listed under *Waiting for your decision*.

  1. Waiting for your decision. Each request shows dates, reason and the cover person.
  2. Approve. You can choose to hand over open tasks to the cover person in one click.
  3. Decline. Add a short note. The person sees it.
  4. Today. Who is away or checked in today.
Good to know
  • Approved leave appears as an *Away until* tag on the Desk.
36

Incentives: rules and monthly awards

FoundersAbout 3 min

Set the rules once, then review awards on the 1st of each month. Nothing is paid without your approval.

  1. + Add rule. Choose *Threshold* (points and on-time %) or *Top N* (reward the top few), who it applies to and the amount.
  2. Edit or pause a rule.
  3. Calculate for this month. Works out who qualifies. Review each award, approve or reject, then mark it paid.
  4. + Manual award. Reward something special, with a reason.
Good to know
  • The system emails founders on the 1st with what is waiting for approval.
37

Scoring weights

FoundersAbout 2 min

On the Scores page, founders see a Scoring settings button (also under Settings → Scoring weights). There they can tune how points are worked out: points per priority, difficulty multipliers, late penalties, the initiative bonus, and the missed-recurring and overdue penalties.

Good to know
  • Each box shows its default. Reset to defaults puts everything back. Changes apply to future scoring; they do not rewrite history.
Part 7

For group owners: several organisations

Running Robokalam, Samaritan Stand-Up, Calvings, Nextpreneur and more from one place, with each one completely separate.

38

Switch between organisations

Group ownersAbout 1 min

Group owners see an organisation switcher at the top of the left menu. Everyone else sees only their own organisation's name.

  1. The switcher. Shows where you are. Click it.
  2. An organisation. Click to move there. The whole screen changes to that organisation's people, tasks and colour.
  3. Manage organisations. Opens the control page.
Good to know
  • What is in one organisation is never visible in another. Staff of Samaritan cannot see Robokalam staff, and the reverse. Only group owners, who are founders of every organisation, cross over.
  • Each time you sign in you start back in your home organisation.
39

Organisations: create, colour and manage

Group ownersAbout 3 min

Open Organisations in the menu. Each organisation has its own card, with its own dashboard.

  1. Your organisation is marked *Viewing now*. *Home* is the one you belong to.
  2. Open dashboard / Open. Jump into that organisation's Desk.
  3. Edit. Rename it, change its one-line description, or pick its brand colour. The app repaints in that colour whenever you are inside it.
  4. New organisation. Start another one.
  5. Group owners. Choose which other founders may open every organisation.
Good to know
  • Archive hides an organisation and signs its people out. Nothing is deleted and you can restore it.
  • People and open-task counts are shown for each organisation, and you can compare at a glance.
40

Start a new organisation

Group ownersAbout 3 min

Adding Calvings, Nextpreneur or anyone else takes about a minute.

  1. Name. For example *Calvings*.
  2. Colour. Pick the brand colour. All options keep text readable.
  3. First founder. Optional but recommended. Enter their name and email, and they receive an invitation. They will only ever see this organisation.
  4. Create. Then open the organisation, add its departments and people just as you did for the first one.
Good to know
  • An email address can belong to only one organisation. If someone works for two, give them a separate email for each, or make them a group owner.
Part 8

Onboarding plan and starter tasks

Hand these out. Tick the boxes on paper, or create the starter tasks in KalamDesk so they appear on each person's My Day.

A

Month 1

  • Ask for leave using the Leave page, naming a cover person

  • Turn on two-step sign-in (founders: required)

  • Send kudos for something genuine

Part 9

Questions and quick reference

Quick answers, and the things worth remembering.

?

Common questions

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in page click Forgot password?, enter your email and use the link we send. It works once and lasts 1 hour.
I never got the invitation email.
Check your spam folder first. Then ask a founder to open your profile and send a new invitation.
I cannot see a menu item a colleague has.
Menus depend on your role. Leads see Desk, Recurring, Dashboard, Stand-ups and Templates. Only founders see Departments, bank details and approvals, and only group owners see Organisations.
Why can't I see colleagues from the other organisation?
Each organisation is completely separate by design. Nobody in one can see the people, tasks or data of another.
My task is late because I am waiting for someone.
Set the status to Blocked and write the reason. The person who assigned it is told, and you avoid the overdue penalty.
I finished a task but it will not close.
It may need proof. Add the note or file it asks for. Or it may be waiting in In Review for a manager to approve.
I changed my mind about a leave request or expense.
Cancel leave on the Leave page, and withdraw an expense claim on the Expenses page, until it has been decided.
Are my bank details safe?
They are encrypted, and only founders can open them. Every reveal asks for a password check and is logged.
Can I use KalamDesk on my phone?
Yes. Open the same address in your phone's browser. The menu becomes a bar along the bottom, and you can add the page to your home screen.
What does a red dot on the bell mean?
A new notification: a task assigned to you, a comment, a reminder or kudos.
I turned on two-step sign-in and lost my phone.
Use one of your recovery codes instead of the 6-digit code. If you have none, another founder can switch it off for you.

Who can do what

Intern / EmployeeTheir own tasks, stand-ups, leave, leads, expenses, scores and kudos. A names-only People directory (no contact details). They can add any teammate to a task as a request.
ManagerEverything above, plus assign work to the team, review, approve leave, Desk, Dashboard.
FounderEverything in the organisation, including people, departments, bank details, expenses and incentives.
Group ownerA founder who can switch between organisations.
